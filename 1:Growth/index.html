Questions

Before you book.

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. We start with a fixed-scope diagnosis. If you don't want to continue, you keep the findings and the plan with no further obligation.

Can you work with our existing marketing person or agency?

Often, yes. We can set strategy and tracking and let your team execute, or run channels ourselves — whichever gets you patients faster.

What does it cost?

The diagnosis is a flat fee scoped to your size. Ongoing work is quoted against the plan so you approve the spend and the expected return before anything starts.

How soon do we see new patients?

Search and reactivation programs typically show booked appointments within 30–60 days. Positioning and referral work compounds over quarters.

Is our data and patient privacy handled properly?

Yes. We work only with de-identified and aggregate data, sign a BAA where relevant, and keep every tool in your practice's own accounts.
