How it works

From search to scheduled.

Every new private patient moves through the same path. One Growth's work is to make each step of it work harder.

  1. Discover

    A patient searches for your speciality in your area.

  2. Consider

    They find your practice and weigh it against the alternatives.

  3. Enquire

    They call, book online, or send a message.

  4. Book

    The enquiry becomes a confirmed appointment.

  5. Attend

    They arrive — and become a patient of the practice.

Impressions and clicks sit before the first step. We're measured on what happens after it.

Questions

Before you book.

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. We start with a fixed-scope capacity assessment. If you'd rather not continue, you keep the findings and the plan with nothing further owed.

What does it cost?

We work backwards from the appointments you want to fill. You approve the media budget and our fee against a projected cost per booked patient before anything goes live.

How soon would we see new patients?

Search campaigns can produce qualified enquiries within the first few weeks. SEO and reputation compound over the following months.

Do you guarantee a number of patients?

No — and be cautious of anyone who does. Your pricing, location, availability, reception and booking process all sit with your practice. We build and run the system designed to produce a predictable flow of qualified enquiries and appointments.

We're close to fully booked — is this for us?

If you're genuinely oversubscribed, not yet. One Growth is for practices with real capacity to fill: a quiet weekday, a second room, a new associate, or a session that could take more.

Is patient data handled properly?

Yes. We work only with de-identified, aggregate data, operate in line with POPIA, and keep every ad account, analytics and tracking tool in your practice's name.
