Questions

Before you book.

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. We start with a fixed-scope capacity assessment. If you'd rather not continue, you keep the findings and the plan with nothing further owed.

What does it cost?

We work backwards from the appointments you want to fill. You approve the media budget and our fee against a projected cost per booked patient before anything goes live.

How soon do we see new patients?

Search campaigns can produce qualified enquiries within the first few weeks. SEO and reputation compound over the following months.

Do you guarantee a number of patients?

No — and treat anyone who does with caution. Too much sits outside our control: your pricing, location, availability, reception, the booking process. We build and optimise a system designed to produce a predictable flow of qualified enquiries and appointments.

We're close to fully booked — is this still worth it?

If you're genuinely oversubscribed, not yet. One Growth is for practices with real capacity to fill: a quiet weekday, a second room, a new associate, or a service line you'd like to grow.

Is patient data and privacy handled properly?

Yes. We work only with de-identified, aggregate data, operate in line with POPIA, and keep every ad account, analytics and tracking tool in your practice's name.
